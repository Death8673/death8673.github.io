Neon Hack Privacy Policy

Neon Hack is published by Death Apps. This policy applies from 16 November 2026.

Game progress

Neon Hack stores your campaign progress, crew, inventory, mission activity, and settings on your device. The release described here has no Neon Hack account or game server. Your device may include local data in Android backups if you enabled them. Save Transfer writes a file to a location you choose. You decide whether to keep or share that file.

Optional rewarded ads

Neon Hack uses Google Mobile Ads to show optional ads that award in-game Stamina. Google can collect and share IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this data in transit. Google's consent tools present applicable privacy choices. Use Settings → Ad Privacy to change those choices when the option is available. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Retention and choices

Local progress stays on the device until you use Reset Save, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. Delete your exported files and Android backups separately if you made copies. Google processes advertising data under its own Privacy Policy.
